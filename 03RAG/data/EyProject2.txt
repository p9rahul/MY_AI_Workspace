Insurance Australia Group Limited (IAG) is a multinational insurance company.
 It is the largest general insurance company in Australia and also the largest in New Zealand 
 through its subsidiary IAG New Zealand. IAG had its origins in the 
 National Roads and Motorists' Association NRMA.
 It is headquartered in Sydney, Australia.
